Conversion
----------
All three are downmixed to mono and resampled to 22.05 kHz with libsndfile +
scipy (polyphase), normalised to about -20 dBFS RMS, peak-limited below 0 dBFS,
and written as 16-bit PCM WAV. The WAVs are embedded in Anaise.exe as RCDATA
(resources 201, 202 and 203) so the executable stays self-contained.

Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
